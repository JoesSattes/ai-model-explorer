Hub Weight Census: what AI models say about themselves

We read only the safetensors headers of thousands of Hugging Face repositories (names, shapes and storage of every tensor, never the weights) and recovered each model's architecture from them. Here is what the models people actually use are made of, how that changed year by year, and where the metadata around them is wrong.
